## How case studies work

1. **Read** the scenario before class (10 minutes).
2. **Discuss** in assigned stakeholder roles — you argue your role's interests, not your own opinion.
3. **Decide** — the class produces a recommendation with trade-offs stated.
4. **Debrief** against the concepts: which lecture's framework decided the case?

Instructor guidance notes (where they exist) are kept in the private instructor materials; everything needed for discussion is on the public pages.
