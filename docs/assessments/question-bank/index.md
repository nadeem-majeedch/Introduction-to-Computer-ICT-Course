# Practice Question Bank — Index

**46 questions** across the ten assessment types · tagged by module, CLO, and difficulty · model answers under each expandable *Model answer* section.

This bank is for **practice and self-assessment**. Graded quiz and exam questions are drawn from separate instructor-only banks and are never published. The questions here assess **understanding rather than recall**: even the multiple-choice items ask *why*, and the scenario items have no answer you could memorize.

---

## 1. How to use the bank

1. **After finishing a module**, attempt its questions with notes closed — the goal is retrieval, not recognition.
2. Write your answer first; then expand the *Model answer* and compare. Where your reasoning differed from the model's, that difference *is* the study point.
3. Scenario and troubleshooting items (types 7, 9, 10) have defensible-answer ranges, not single answers — the model answers explain what a good answer must contain.
4. Bring disagreements to office hours: a question you can argue with is a question that taught you something.

## 2. Question types and difficulty tags

Every question carries a tag line like `Module 3 · CLO-3 · Difficulty 2/4 · Type: Short answer`.

**Difficulty scale (1–4)** — aligned with the case-bank levels:

| Tag | Meaning | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| 1/4 | Recall + direct application | One lecture's ideas, applied straight |
| 2/4 | Multi-step application | Combine two ideas; show a method |
| 3/4 | Comparative / scenario reasoning | Weigh options; justify against criteria |
| 4/4 | Extended problem-solving | Multiple interacting constraints; defensible-design answers |

**The ten types**, and where each maps to graded components:

| Type | Practises | Examined in |
|---|---|---|
| Multiple choice (with why) | Fast concept checks | Quizzes, exams |
| True/false with explanation | Precision of understanding | Quizzes, exams |
| Short answer | Definitions in your own words | Quizzes, exams |
| Conceptual questions | Mechanisms, not slogans | Exams |
| Scenario-based questions | Transfer to new situations | Labs, exam scenario item |
| Troubleshooting questions | Isolation method | Labs, midterm |
| Data representation exercises | Conversions, encodings, overflow | Midterm, final |
| Networking & cybersecurity scenarios | Layered reasoning, safe responses | Final |
| Comparative questions | Criteria-based judgement | Final, case bank |
| Extended problem-solving | Full toolkit | Final scenario, project, case bank L3–L4 |

## 4. Answer policy

- Every question has a model answer or a marking-scheme statement, written to be checkable by you and by the validator (`scripts/validate_assessment_pack.py` verifies presence and non-stub status).
- Model answers describe **what a good answer contains**, including where alternatives are defensible — they are study material, not a substitute for attempting the question.
- Found an error in a model answer? Report it — the course fixes and re-validates. Disputes about model answers are legitimate study activity.
