# Integrated Project — Milestones

> **Brief:** [brief.md](brief.md) · **Rubric:** [rubric.md](rubric.md)

Four checkpoints spread across the semester keep the project small, real, and finished. Each milestone is graded (see weights below) and returned with feedback.

---

## Milestone 1 — Proposal (due L16, 10% of the 25%)

**Deliverable:** one page.

1. Track chosen ([A–D](brief.md#choose-one-track)); pairing status.
2. Problem statement in two sentences: *for whom, what pain, what changes.*
3. Artefact sketch: what exists at the showcase (be concrete — "a spreadsheet with X, Y, Z sheets").
4. The four modules you'll integrate, one line each.
5. The single biggest risk to finishing (be honest; risk quality is graded).

**Feedback returned:** track/scope approval or a narrowing request — the scope guard starts here.

## Milestone 2 — Design document (due L24, 10%)

**Deliverable:** 2–3 pages.

1. **Architecture of the artefact:** sheets/files/sections and what each contains (track A/B/C as fits).
2. **Module map:** the four modules with a concrete contribution each ("M4: colour codes stored as `#RRGGBB` hex, validated by formula").
3. **One flowchart** (L31 standards) of the artefact's main logic or the study's pipeline.
4. **Data plan** (track B): source, license, cleaning steps planned, privacy check.
5. **Work plan:** three dated checkpoints for the remaining weeks.

**Feedback returned:** design review — feasibility and integration depth, before you build the wrong thing well.

## Milestone 3 — Working check (during Module 7 week, 15%)

**Deliverable:** 5-minute in-class or LMS-video demo of the artefact *as it stands* — rough edges welcome, vaporware penalized.

**What's checked:** the core mechanism works (formulas compute, charts render, data pipeline runs, slides play); integration is visibly underway; the module map from M2 is still accurate (or honestly revised).

## Milestone 4 — Showcase + final report (L32, 65%)

**Deliverables:**

1. **Live showcase presentation** (8–10 minutes): the artefact demonstrated live, following L20 slide standards; every team member explains part of it.
2. **Final report** (6–10 pages): problem, design decisions with the module map, what you built, limitations stated honestly, what you'd do next, and the M2 flowchart updated to reality.
3. **Peer-feedback slips** from the audience are part of the showcase rubric.

**Weights within the 65%:** artefact quality & integration 30% · presentation 20% · final report 15%.

## Schedule summary

| 1 Proposal | L16 | 10% | 1 page |
