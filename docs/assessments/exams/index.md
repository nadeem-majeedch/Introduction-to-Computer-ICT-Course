# Exam Blueprints — Midterm and Final

> These blueprints are **published in advance**: they state exactly what each exam covers, how marks are distributed, and how the timing works. The actual questions are not published; graded questions are drawn from separate instructor-only banks. Weightings are proposed and await instructor approval (OQ-13).

---

## Midterm blueprint

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Timing** | 90 minutes, in class, Week 9 (after L16) |
| **Coverage** | Modules 1–4 (L01–L16) |
| **Purpose** | Confirm Stage 1–2 skills (foundations, hardware, software/OS, data representation) before the deeper modules build on them |
| **Format** | Three sections, in the proportions below |
| **Materials** | Closed book; calculators not required (arithmetic is deliberately small); no devices |

### Section plan and mark allocation (100 marks)

| Section | Items | Marks | Difficulty mix | Time guide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Short conceptual** | ~12 short answers + ~8 objective items | 40 | mostly 1/4–2/4 | ~35 min |
| **B — Worked problems** | 4 problems (conversions with method; two's complement; truth table; memory-budget allocation) | 40 | 2/4, one 3/4 | ~40 min |
| **C — Mini-scenario** | 1 integrative scenario (diagnose → order fixes → plan) | 20 | 3/4 | ~15 min |

### What Section B's method marks mean

Every worked problem is marked **method first**: a correct final answer with no shown method earns at most half, because the transferable skill is the method (repeated division, inversion+1, intermediate truth-table columns). Intermediate columns shown but never summed still earn method marks; bare answers do not.

### Coverage guarantee

Every CLO-1…CLO-4 appears at least twice across the paper (see the [assessment framework's coverage note](../index.md)); logic (CLO-5) is *not* midterm-assessed except as a clearly-labelled bonus stretch item. The published blueprint's section list is exactly what appears in the room — no surprise sections.

## Final blueprint

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Timing** | 120 minutes, university exam period |
| **Coverage** | Cumulative; **emphasis on Modules 5–8 (~75% of marks)**, Modules 1–4 as feeder items (~25%) |
| **Format** | Three sections in the proportions below |
| **Materials** | Closed book; a formula-free exam (no formulas beyond course-published ones exist in this course) |

### Section plan and mark allocation (100 marks)

| Section | Items | Marks | Difficulty mix | Time guide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Concepts (Modules 5–8 weighted)** | ~20 items incl. ~4 feeder items on Modules 1–4 | 40 | 1/4–3/4 | ~40 min |
| **B — Problems** | 5–6 problems (truth tables/adders, spreadsheet design, SQL reading/writing, flowchart, conversion feeder) | 35 | 2/4–3/4 | ~45 min |
| **C — Long scenario (choice of two variants)** | 1 integrative scenario-response — variants are announced *on the paper*, choose one | 25 | 4/4 | ~35 min |

### The Section C scenario

Both variants assess the same skill — structured analysis of an integrated situation (e.g., a security incident at a small organization, or an AI-adoption dilemma in a student-services office). Marks credit **ordering and rationale** (a fix list without rationale earns half), the use of course frameworks (isolation method, 3-2-1, verification loop, data-protection principles), and honest calibration of claims. The rubric is published in the [rubrics pack](../rubrics.md#exam-scenario-rubric).

### Coverage guarantee

CLO-5…CLO-10 carry primary evidence; CLO-1…CLO-4 appear as feeder items so cumulative retention stays honest; the project (CLO-11) is examined *separately* — the final's only synthesis item is explicitly marked and unambiguous. Every CLO has graded evidence in at least two components across the course (framework coverage note).

## Academic integrity at exams

- Closed-book, no devices; invigilated per university procedure.
- Question pools are large and partially refreshed each semester; sharing exam content between sections or semesters is a violation.
- A suspected integrity issue is processed by the syllabus's procedure — evidence examined, presumption of good faith; technical course errors (e.g., an answer-key mistake) are corrected and communicated, never attributed to students.
- **Before the exam:** the question bank's difficulty-3 and difficulty-4 items, and the case bank's Level 2–3 cases, are the closest legal proxies for exam questions — practise there, not with "past papers" from older cohorts (none are official).
