# Assessment

This page explains every graded component, how each is marked, and the policies that protect fairness. Answer keys and item banks are **not** published; you receive feedback on every graded attempt.

---

## 1. Weighting overview

| Component | Weight | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Quizzes | 15% | Best 10 of 14 weekly in-lecture quizzes |
| Labs | 20% | 8 graded lab handouts |
| Midterm examination | 20% | In-class, after L16 (Modules 1–4) |
| Integrated project | 25% | Proposal → design → working check → showcase + report |
| Final examination | 20% | Comprehensive; emphasis on Modules 5–8 |

## 2. Quizzes (15%)

- **Format:** 5–10 minutes, 4–6 short questions on the two most recent lectures.
- **Frequency:** 14 are offered; your best **10** count (this absorbs ordinary bad weeks — no make-ups needed for single misses).
- **Preparation:** each lecture's *Check your understanding* questions mirror quiz style; [practice quizzes](assessments/quizzes/index.md) give instant self-check feedback.
- **Feedback:** graded quizzes are returned in the following lecture with common-mistakes discussion.

## 3. Labs (20%)

One lab per module — see [Labs](labs/index.md). Grading uses criteria published on each lab handout (no hidden criteria). Typical rubric dimensions:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Correct completion of required tasks | 60% |
| Working demonstration or artifact | 25% |
| Reflection questions answered thoughtfully | 15% |

- **Late policy:** 10% deduction per day late.
- **Collaboration:** you may discuss approach with classmates; every submission must be your own execution.

## 4. Midterm examination (20%)

- **Coverage:** Modules 1–4 (L01–L16).
- **Format:** short conceptual questions + small worked problems (a conversion, a truth table, a file-management task description).
- **Preparation:** quizzes Q01–Q09, lab reflections, lecture *Check your understanding* items.

## 5. Integrated project (25%)

The project turns course concepts into a working artifact of your choice within provided tracks. Full details:

- [Project brief](assessments/project/brief.md) — tracks, requirements, constraints
- [Milestones](assessments/project/milestones.md) — proposal (L08/L16 window), design (L24), working check (L24), showcase (L32)
- [Rubric](assessments/project/rubric.md) — exactly how the 25% is earned

## 6. Final examination (20%)

- **Coverage:** comprehensive, with emphasis on Modules 5–8.
- **Format:** conceptual questions, small problems (logic, numbers, networks), one scenario-response question (security or AI literacy).

## 7. Grades and integrity

- Grade boundaries and the appeals process follow institutional policy; the university's grading scale is published before Week 1.
- All submitted work must be your own; AI-tool use is governed per assessment (see the [Syllabus](syllabus.md#7-course-policies) and L28).
- The university's academic-integrity procedure applies to all components.
