# Complete Teaching Blueprint — 32 Lectures

**Companion to:** `01-course-architecture.md` · **Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 2026-09-19
**Scope:** session-level teaching plan for every lecture (2 hours each, 64 contact hours). Lecture-level *content* lives in `docs/lectures/L01–L32`; this document is the **instructor's delivery blueprint** and stays out of the published site.

**Consistency rule:** the objectives, vocabulary, and CLO mappings below are condensed from the published lecture pages. If a lecture page changes, this blueprint changes in the same commit. `scripts/validate_teaching_blueprint.py` enforces the machine-checkable subset (32 rows per module block, 64-hour total, CLO parity with front-matter, one-time objective coverage).

## 1. Teaching structure — the default 2-hour session

Every lecture follows one time-boxed template. Deviations are planned, not improvised: activity-driven lectures (L02, L13, L17, L22, L29) and showcase week (L32) note their variations in the plan tables.

| Segment | Time | Purpose | Standard technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engagement & prior-knowledge activation | 10 min | Surface what students already know; connect to last lecture | Opening question, quick poll, 2-minute pair recall of last session's key terms |
| Concept explanation 1 | 25 min | First concept block of the session | Board + slides, worked example early, jargon defined on first use |
| Demonstration / visualization | 15 min | Make the abstract concrete | Live demo, diagram build, simulator, or case-study segment |
| Concept explanation 2 | 20 min | Second concept block (deeper or applied) | Continues from the demo; students predict before reveal |
| Guided practice | 15 min | Students try the skill with support | Worksheet, simulator steps, conversion drill, or lab kickoff |
| Problem-solving activity | 10 min | Apply concepts to an unfamiliar twist | Pairs solve a scenario; one pair presents |
| Formative assessment | 10 min | Check learning while it can still be fixed | Exit-ticket questions from the lecture's *Check your understanding* pool |
| Summary & exit ticket | 5 min | Consolidate; bridge to next lecture | One-sentence summary per student or 3-2-1 slip; homework assigned |

**Load discipline:** two concept blocks only. If a topic list will not fit, it moves to the homework column or the following lecture — the module tables below are already trimmed to this budget. The 10-minute formative segment uses questions that mirror quiz style, so weekly quizzes (best 10 of 14) measure the same skills students practise.

**Material sources:** slides and demos are built from the lecture page's sections; activities reference `docs/activities/`; lab kickoffs reference `docs/labs/`; case studies reference `docs/case-studies/`. Graded instruments and keys stay in `instructor/`.

**No duplicate lecture objectives:** each lecture's objectives are distinct; shared strands appear as *progressions*, not repeats — e.g., security appears in L12 (safe install), L23 (safe browsing), L29 (threats/defences), L30 (privacy) with non-overlapping objectives; productivity appears in L19 (documents/spreadsheets) vs L20 (presentations/workflows); logic in L17 (expressions/tables) vs L18 (gates/circuits). `scripts/validate_teaching_blueprint.py` enforces objective-line uniqueness across the 32 plans.

**First-semester difficulty:** stage gating holds — Weeks 1–8 stay in Stages 1–2 (describe/identify, represent/manipulate); construction and evaluation (Stages 3–4) arrive only after concrete artefacts exist. Each session carries at most two concept blocks (§1 load discipline); everything else is homework, lab, or later lecture.
